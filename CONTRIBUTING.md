# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in our work at Our World in Data!

## External pull requests

**We do not accept pull requests from anyone outside the Our World in Data team.**
This policy applies to the entire repository, including the Grapher visualisation
framework and the Our World in Data site. Please do not submit external pull requests.

## Reporting issues and suggesting improvements

You are welcome to [report an issue](https://github.com/owid/owid-grapher/issues)
or [start a discussion](https://github.com/owid/owid-grapher/discussions) to
report bugs, suggest improvements, or share feedback.

If you prefer to report an issue privately, email info@ourworldindata.org.

## Reporting security vulnerabilities

Please report security vulnerabilities privately through the repository's
[GitHub Advisories page](https://github.com/owid/owid-grapher/security/advisories)
by clicking **Report a vulnerability**, as described in our
[security policy](SECURITY.md). Do not disclose vulnerabilities in public issues
or discussions.
